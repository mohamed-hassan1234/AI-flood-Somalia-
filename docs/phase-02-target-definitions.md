# Phase 02 Target Definitions

**Version:** 1.1.0

**Frozen before final-test evaluation:** yes

These targets are defined from the Phase 01 evidence before inspecting Phase 02 final-test results.
They are separate operational questions, not one generic risk label.

## Drought

The drought track predicts **agricultural vegetation stress in the next MOD13Q1 16-day composite**
for a canonical district. A positive label is strict-QA future NDVI at least one standard deviation
below its district/seasonal-slot reference; a known value above that threshold is negative; absent or
QA-null future vegetation is unknown and excluded. The reference mean and standard deviation are
fixed from 2015–2020, the outer training era, and do not use validation/test vegetation.

This is not the circular statement “drought equals low rainfall.” Rainfall, POWER wetness and
temperature, and already-available vegetation are predictors; future vegetation is only the label.
A MODIS composite is considered available after its 16-day support period. Version 1.1 corrects the
rejected v1.0 prototype, which inherited a rank-based composite number that shifted after provider
gaps and therefore misaligned seasons. The target describes an agricultural/environmental condition
and must not be interpreted as humanitarian impact or hunger.

## Flood

The flood track predicts whether a gauge will meet or exceed its official **moderate river level
threshold during days t+1 through t+3**. The unit is one of SH001, SH002, SH004, JB001, or JB009.
All three future dates must be observed for a known negative; otherwise the target is unknown.

This is a riverine early-warning target for five supported Jubba/Shabelle monitoring corridors. It
does not represent flash/surface flooding or every Somalia district. Current/recent level, rate of
rise, antecedent rain, temperature, and wetness end at t. Future level is label metadata only.

## Food security

The food-security track predicts, 30 days before an observed current IPC validity period starts,
whether **at least 20% of a canonical region's population is in IPC Phase 3 or worse**. The source is
the IPC Level 1 `current` Phase `3+` percentage. First and second projections are excluded from labels.

The unit remains IPC Level 1/canonical region; it is not forced into district labels. Historical IPC
aliases are resolved with the Phase 01 crosswalk. Previous observed IPC is usable only when its
validity period ended by the as-of date. All climate, vegetation, and market features are similarly
cut off. The 20% burden threshold operationalizes a high regional Crisis-or-Worse burden; it is not
presented as an official IPC area classification generated by this model.

## Unknowns, missingness, and partitions

Unknown labels are never converted to negative. Missing predictors are retained and handled inside
each training-only preprocessing pipeline with explicit missingness indicators. No imputation
statistic is learned from validation or test data.

Fixed outer partitions are chronological:

| Track | Training | Validation | Untouched final test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drought | 2015–2020 | 2021–2022 | 2023–2025 |
| Flood | 2015–2020 | 2021–2022 | 2023–2025 |
| Food security | 2017–2021 | 2022–2023 | 2024–2025 |

Rolling expanding-window folds are used for historical backtesting. The final test is evaluated once
after target, feature, candidate, calibration, threshold, and acceptance definitions are frozen.

The machine-readable contract is `ml/config/targets.json`; predeclared acceptance rules are in
`ml/config/acceptance.json`.
